# Shopizer Living Specifications

Status: proposed maintenance model; no automatic sync is implemented.

## Source of Truth

Shopizer source code and tests define observed implementation behavior. Approved business policy must come from designated stakeholders; generated drafts are not authoritative on their own.

## Update Triggers

- Refresh source analysis when the pinned Shopizer commit changes or a relevant API/service/test changes.
- Reconcile requirement and test links after an approved contract change.
- Preserve prior review decisions and identify generated-vs-human-authored sections.

## Review and Ownership

Assign owners for order, cart, and customer artifacts; review security/privacy claims with the appropriate maintainers. Define retention and history policy before automating updates.

## Traceability Gate

Before publishing consolidated outputs, verify each requirement has source evidence or an approved policy reference, linked design/test artifacts, and an explicit verification status.
